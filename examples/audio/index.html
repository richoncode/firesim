Fire voice

A roar and a crackle, synthesized in the browser. Nothing is sampled from a recording. The simulation on the playback page is Daniel Greenheck’s Fire Pro. This page has no simulation: the slider is the fire.

Unmute first. Browsers block audio until a click or a key. Return Level to zero and the crackle dies out. U toggles mute.
